Modes 語言與語氣模式

Three language modes and two funny-level sliders decide what language you read and how playful the voice is — never what the facts say.

三種語言模式加兩條搞笑程度滑桿，決定你讀乜嘢話、把聲幾好玩 —— 但永遠唔會改事實。

Behaviour 行為

Language modes: English, Chinese (Hong Kong), or bilingual with both tracks side by side. Both tracks ship inside the app; nothing downloads at runtime. Missing strings fall back to English rather than rendering blank.

Funny levels: two sliders from 1 (fully serious) to 5 (maximum playfulness), shipped at 5, one per language. The level styles the voice of messages, tooltips and notifications — every category including errors — while versions, paths and causes stay exact.

School mode presents English only and suppresses playful copy and novelty surfaces such as the dim-sum surprise; the unlock ladder starts directly at the arithmetic rung.

語言模式：英文、中文（香港）、或者雙語並排。兩套文案都打包喺app入面，執行期唔使下載；缺字會退返英文而唔會留白。搞笑程度：兩條滑桿各管一種語言，1（全正經）到5（最好玩），預設5。佢影響所有訊息類別嘅語氣 —— 包括錯誤 —— 但版本、路徑、成因照樣準確。返學模式淨係出英文，收埋搞笑文案同點心驚喜呢類趣味表面；解鎖梯階直接由算術嗰關開始。

Configuration 設定

All three controls live in settings and persist locally: language mode, English funny level, Chinese funny level, School mode toggle. This documentation site implements the same contract for its own chrome — see its Settings page.

三個控制都喺設定入面而且本地保存：語言模式、英文搞笑程度、中文搞笑程度、返學模式開關。呢個文檔網站自己嘅介面都實踐同一套契約 —— 見佢嘅設定頁。

Failure modes 失敗模式

  • Missing Chinese string → English fallback, never blank space.
  • Slider values outside 1–5 clamp instead of breaking copy selection.
  • School-mode enforcement is completing surface by surface; any gap is tracked openly rather than hidden.

中文缺字 → 退英文，唔會空白；滑桿超出1–5會夾返入嚟；返學模式逐個表面補完中，任何缺口都會公開追蹤而唔會收埋。

Security 安全考量

Language and tone preferences carry no sensitive data and never leave the machine. Tone never changes factual content: errors identify the real failure and remedy at every level.

語言同語氣偏好唔含敏感資料，亦永不離機。語氣永不改動事實：每級語氣之下，錯誤訊息照樣指出真正成因同補救方法。

Verification 驗證方法

Switch through all three modes and spot-check navigation, dialogs and notifications; move each slider between 1 and 5 and confirm tone changes while facts stay identical; restart and confirm persistence; enable School mode and confirm English presentation plus suppression of playful surfaces.

三個模式逐一轉，抽查導航、對話框、通知；兩條滑桿由1推到5，確認語氣變咗但事實一字不差；重啟確認保存；開返學模式確認全英文兼收埋趣味表面。

Authenticator 驗證器與保險庫

An encrypted vault for secrets, a memory-hashed app lock, and a built-in TOTP authenticator surface in progress.

秘密收喺加密保險庫、程式鎖用記憶體困難雜湊，仲有興建中建中嘅內置TOTP驗證器。

Behaviour 行為

The main process owns a vault for sensitive values such as provider keys. Encryption uses Electron safeStorage — DPAPI-backed on Windows, binding ciphertext to your user profile. Values are addressed by opaque identifiers; consumers hold references, never contents.

The app lock derives a verifier with scrypt, a memory-hard KDF, so the stored verifier does not reverse into your secret. Unlocking gates vault-backed surfaces. A TOTP authenticator screen — register RFC 6238 entries, read rotating codes computed locally — is being built by the Authenticator lane.

保險庫由主程序持有，收供應商金鑰呢類敏感值。加密用Electron safeStorage —— Windows上DPAPI撐住，密文綁住你嘅用戶profile。數值以不透明識別碼尋址；使用方只持參考、唔持內容。程式鎖用scrypt（記憶體困難KDF）導出驗證值，所以儲存嘅驗證值逆向唔返你嘅秘密；解鎖把守所有依賴保險庫嘅表面。TOTP驗證器畫面 —— 登記RFC 6238條目、本地計算輪換碼 —— 正由Authenticator lane興建。

Configuration 設定

Setting or changing the app lock happens in settings; the verifier is stored, never the secret. Vault entries appear when a feature stores a secret and disappear when their owner deletes them.

設定或者更改程式鎖都喺設定入面做；儲存嘅係驗證值，永遠唔係秘密本身。某功能存秘密嗰陣保險庫條目就會出現，擁有者刪除佢哋嗰陣就會消失。

Failure modes 失敗模式

  • If OS encryption is unavailable, writes fail with an explicit error rather than storing anything unprotected.
  • A forgotten lock secret cannot be recovered from the verifier — by design.
  • A dangling keyRef fails closed with a credential error and can be cleaned from the vault.

作業系統加密不可用時，寫入會如實報錯，唔會裸存任何嘢；鎖密碼唔見咗，由驗證值係救唔返 —— 呢個係設計；懸空keyRef會fail-closed彈憑證錯誤，之後可以清走。

Security 安全考量

Secrets are encrypted at rest with an OS-bound mechanism and never written to config files, exports, logs or history entries. scrypt makes brute-forcing a stolen verifier expensive. Redaction applies everywhere. Treat your Windows account as the boundary: DPAPI ties decryption to it, so protect the account.

靜態秘密用作業系統綁定嘅機制加密，永不落入配置檔、匯出、日誌或者歷史。scrypt令偷到驗證值想暴力破解都要付出大代價。遮蔽原則放諸四海。你個Windows帳戶就係邊界：DPAPI將解密綁落去，所以帳戶本身要顧好。

Verification 驗證方法

Save a provider key and confirm the record holds a keyRef while the vault holds ciphertext, end-to-end requests succeed, and no log or export shows secret material. Set the lock, restart, confirm gating. Authenticator test vectors land with the finished surface.

存一把供應商金鑰：確認紀錄只有keyRef、保險庫只有密文、請求端到端成功、日誌同匯出不見任何秘密材料。設鎖、重啟、確認把守有效。驗證器嘅標準測試向量會連同完成品一起交付。

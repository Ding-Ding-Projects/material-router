Appearance 外觀自訂

Material Design 3 throughout: light/dark/system themes today, with per-element editors, presets and export/import as the Appearance lane lands them.

全套Material Design 3：今日已有淺色／深色／跟系統主題；逐元素編輯、預設集、匯入匯出由Appearance lane陸續送上。

Behaviour 行為

Shipped: light, dark and system themes from one token sheet applied to every surface — window chrome, tab strip, palette, notifications — switching together and persisting across restarts.

In progress by the Appearance lane: per-element appearance editors opened from any element’s context menu (with keyboard equivalents), covering fonts, sizes, weights, colours, spacing, radii, icons and states with per-property and global resets; density, accent/seed colour and font selection with live preview; contrast readouts inside colour controls.

已交付：淺色、深色、跟系統三個主題，出自同一張token表，覆蓋所有表面 —— 窗框、分頁列、指令面板、通知 —— 一齊切換、重啟保存。Appearance lane施工中：任何元素嘅右鍵菜單（連鍵盤等效路徑）都可以打開逐元素外觀編輯器，涵蓋字體、字號、字重、顏色、間距、弧度、圖示、狀態，支援逐屬性同全局重置；仲有密度、重點色、字體選擇連即時預覽，以及色彩控制入面嘅對比度讀數。

Configuration 設定

Theme selection lives in app settings and persists locally. Appearance preferences are per-user, local-only and never synchronized. On this site, theme/accent/density/typography controls live in Settings → Appearance, persisted in browser storage.

主題選擇喺app設定入面，本地保存。外觀偏好屬於每位使用者、只留喺本機、永不同步。本網站嘅主題／重點色／密度／字體排版控制喺「設定 → 外觀」，存喺瀏覽器storage。

Failure modes 失敗模式

  • An unreadable preference falls back to the shipped default theme rather than leaving surfaces unstyled.
  • Following the system theme means the app moves when the OS does; pin a fixed theme if that is unwanted.
  • Custom colours that harm legibility get flagged by contrast readouts once those controls ship.

偏好檔讀唔到就退回出廠主題，唔會令界面冇樣式；跟系統主題即係作業系統變你就變，唔想就跟死一個固定主題；有害可讀性嘅自訂色，等對比度讀數推出後會被標示。

Security 安全考量

Appearance preferences contain no sensitive data, are stored with the rest of the app’s local settings, and nothing about them leaves the machine.

外觀偏好不含敏感資料，同其他本地設定一齊儲存，相關資訊永不離機。

Verification 驗證方法

Switch light/dark/system and confirm every visible surface updates together; restart and confirm persistence; flip the OS theme while set to system and confirm the app follows.

淺色／深色／系統逐一切換，確認所有可見表面同步更新；重啟確認保存；設成「系統」之後反轉作業系統主題，確認app跟得上。
